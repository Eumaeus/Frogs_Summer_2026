# Important Things to Know When Reading Aristophanes' *Frogs*: An introduction

One may think comedies are just funny, but comedies are loaded with politic drama and literary power that packs an explanatory punch. Aristophanes' *Frogs* written/performed in 405 BCE encompasses all relative matters concerning the Athenians/Greeks of the 5th Century. *Frogs* contains wit at it's fanged finest. A comedy that brings to light the theater of tragedians from the perspective of an original comedian. *Frogs* ruminates on the politics, religions/cults, and the ignorance of pedagogy in the 5th Century. Theater, especially, Comedy connected the Athenian masses (Aristocrat, mercenary, poet, shopkeeper, rustic, parasite, woman, man, child, tutor, and slave) bringing them together to observe a spectacle.
